Can my GPU run Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B?

Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B has 2.45T parameters, about 122B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 4.45 TiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs more than 16 × H100 80 GB, more than 16 × H200 141 GB, more than 16 × B300 288 GB and more than 16 × L40S 48 GB. It doesn't fit on any single GPU, even in INT4.

Questions

How much VRAM does Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B need?

The weights take 4.45 TiB as published (BF16), 4.45 TiB in BF16, 2.27 TiB in FP8 and 1.18 TiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 96.4 KiB per token: 3.15 GiB for one 32K-token request and 11.8 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (1.18 TiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs more than 16 H100s.

Can I run Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. It's too large even for a 512 GB Mac Studio in INT4.

How much context can Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B handle on 16 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 256K tokens (the model's limit is 256K). At 32K tokens per request, about 65 requests fit in memory at once.
