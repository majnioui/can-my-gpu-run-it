Can my GPU run DeepSeek-V3.1?

DeepSeek-V3.1 has 685B parameters, about 51.1B active per token. As published in FP8 the weights take 641 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 16 × H100 80 GB, 8 × H200 141 GB, 4 × B300 288 GB and more than 16 × L40S 48 GB. Quantized to INT4, it fits on a single Mac M3 Ultra 512 GB.

Questions

How much VRAM does DeepSeek-V3.1 need?

The weights take 641 GiB as published (FP8), 1.25 TiB in BF16, 652 GiB in FP8 and 340 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 68.6 KiB per token: 2.14 GiB for one 32K-token request and 8.58 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can DeepSeek-V3.1 run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (340 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 16 H100s.

Can I run DeepSeek-V3.1 on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it in INT4, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can DeepSeek-V3.1 handle on 8 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 160K tokens (the model's limit is 160K). At 32K tokens per request, about 20 requests fit in memory at once.
