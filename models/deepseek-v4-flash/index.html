Can my GPU run DeepSeek-V4-Flash?

DeepSeek-V4-Flash has 291B parameters, about 20.4B active per token. As published in FP4 the weights take 149 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 4 × H100 80 GB, 2 × H200 141 GB, 1 × B300 288 GB and 4 × L40S 48 GB. It fits on a single B200 180 GB, B300 288 GB, MI300X 192 GB or MI355X 288 GB without quantizing.

Questions

How much VRAM does DeepSeek-V4-Flash need?

The weights take 149 GiB as published (FP4), 542 GiB in BF16, 277 GiB in FP8 and 145 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 6.1 KiB per token: 0.19 GiB for one 32K-token request and 0.76 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can DeepSeek-V4-Flash run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (145 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 4 H100s.

Can I run DeepSeek-V4-Flash on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it as published, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can DeepSeek-V4-Flash handle on 2 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 1M tokens (the model's limit is 1M). At 32K tokens per request, about 257 requests fit in memory at once.
