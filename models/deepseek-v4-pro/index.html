Can my GPU run DeepSeek-V4-Pro?

DeepSeek-V4-Pro has 1.60T parameters, about 75.6B active per token. As published in FP4 the weights take 805 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 16 × H100 80 GB, 8 × H200 141 GB, 4 × B300 288 GB and more than 16 × L40S 48 GB. It doesn't fit on any single GPU, even in INT4.

Questions

How much VRAM does DeepSeek-V4-Pro need?

The weights take 805 GiB as published (FP4), 2.91 TiB in BF16, 1.48 TiB in FP8 and 792 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 8.8 KiB per token: 0.28 GiB for one 32K-token request and 1.09 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can DeepSeek-V4-Pro run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (792 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 16 H100s.

Can I run DeepSeek-V4-Pro on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. It's too large even for a 512 GB Mac Studio in INT4.

How much context can DeepSeek-V4-Pro handle on 8 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 1M tokens (the model's limit is 1M). At 32K tokens per request, about 84 requests fit in memory at once.
