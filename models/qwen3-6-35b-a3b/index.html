Can my GPU run Qwen3.6-35B-A3B?

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B has 36.0B parameters, about 4.7B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 67.0 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 1 × H100 80 GB, 1 × H200 141 GB, 1 × B300 288 GB and 2 × L40S 48 GB. It fits on a single H100 80 GB, H200 141 GB, B200 180 GB or B300 288 GB without quantizing.

Questions

How much VRAM does Qwen3.6-35B-A3B need?

The weights take 67.0 GiB as published (BF16), 67.0 GiB in BF16, 35.1 GiB in FP8 and 19.1 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 20.5 KiB per token: 0.66 GiB for one 32K-token request and 2.53 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can Qwen3.6-35B-A3B run on a single H100?

Yes. As published in BF16 it fits on one H100 80 GB with 32K tokens of context.

Can I run Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

On one RTX 4090 (24 GB) it fits in INT4 with 32K of context. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it as published, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can Qwen3.6-35B-A3B handle on 1 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 256K tokens (the model's limit is 256K). At 32K tokens per request, about 87 requests fit in memory at once.
