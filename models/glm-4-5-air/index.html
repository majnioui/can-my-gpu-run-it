Can my GPU run GLM-4.5-Air?

GLM-4.5-Air has 110B parameters, about 17.0B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 206 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 4 × H100 80 GB, 2 × H200 141 GB, 1 × B300 288 GB and 8 × L40S 48 GB. It fits on a single B300 288 GB, MI355X 288 GB or Mac M3 Ultra 512 GB without quantizing.

Questions

How much VRAM does GLM-4.5-Air need?

The weights take 206 GiB as published (BF16), 206 GiB in BF16, 106 GiB in FP8 and 56.2 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 184.0 KiB per token: 5.75 GiB for one 32K-token request and 23.0 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can GLM-4.5-Air run on a single H100?

Not as published, but it does in INT4 or FP4. As published in BF16 it needs 4 H100s.

Can I run GLM-4.5-Air on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it as published, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can GLM-4.5-Air handle on 2 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 128K tokens (the model's limit is 128K). At 32K tokens per request, about 7 requests fit in memory at once.
