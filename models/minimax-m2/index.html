Can my GPU run MiniMax-M2?

MiniMax-M2 has 229B parameters, about 11.0B active per token. As published in FP8 the weights take 214 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 4 × H100 80 GB, 2 × H200 141 GB, 1 × B300 288 GB and 8 × L40S 48 GB. It fits on a single B300 288 GB, MI355X 288 GB or Mac M3 Ultra 512 GB without quantizing.

Questions

How much VRAM does MiniMax-M2 need?

The weights take 214 GiB as published (FP8), 426 GiB in BF16, 218 GiB in FP8 and 115 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 248.0 KiB per token: 7.75 GiB for one 32K-token request and 31.0 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can MiniMax-M2 run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (115 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 4 H100s.

Can I run MiniMax-M2 on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it as published, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can MiniMax-M2 handle on 2 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 143K tokens (the model's limit is 192K). At 32K tokens per request, about 4 requests fit in memory at once.
