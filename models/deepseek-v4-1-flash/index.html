Can my GPU run DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash?

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash has 763B parameters, about 31.5B active per token. As published in FP8 the weights take 475 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 8 × H100 80 GB, 4 × H200 141 GB, 2 × B300 288 GB and 16 × L40S 48 GB. It doesn't fit on any single GPU, even in INT4.

Questions

How much VRAM does DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash need?

The weights take 475 GiB as published (FP8), 1.39 TiB in BF16, 726 GiB in FP8 and 379 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 32.7 KiB per token: 1.02 GiB for one 32K-token request and 4.08 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (379 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 8 H100s.

Can I run DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. It's too large even for a 512 GB Mac Studio in INT4.

How much context can DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash handle on 4 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 176K tokens (the model's limit is 1M). At 32K tokens per request, about 5 requests fit in memory at once.
