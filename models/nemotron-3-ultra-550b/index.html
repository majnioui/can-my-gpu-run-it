Can my GPU run Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B?

Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B has 561B parameters, about 67.3B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 1.02 TiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 16 × H100 80 GB, 16 × H200 141 GB, 8 × B300 288 GB and more than 16 × L40S 48 GB. Quantized to INT4, it fits on a single Mac M3 Ultra 512 GB.

Questions

How much VRAM does Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B need?

The weights take 1.02 TiB as published (BF16), 1.02 TiB in BF16, 534 GiB in FP8 and 280 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 18.0 KiB per token: 0.75 GiB for one 32K-token request and 1.88 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (280 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 16 H100s.

Can I run Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it in INT4, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B handle on 16 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 256K tokens (the model's limit is 256K). At 32K tokens per request, about 280 requests fit in memory at once.
