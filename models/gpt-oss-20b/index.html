Can my GPU run gpt-oss-20b?

gpt-oss-20b has 20.9B parameters, about 4.2B active per token. As published in FP4 the weights take 12.8 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 1 × H100 80 GB, 1 × H200 141 GB, 1 × B300 288 GB and 1 × L40S 48 GB. It fits on a single H100 80 GB, H200 141 GB, B200 180 GB or B300 288 GB without quantizing.

Questions

How much VRAM does gpt-oss-20b need?

The weights take 12.8 GiB as published (FP4), 39.0 GiB in BF16, 20.9 GiB in FP8 and 11.9 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 24.0 KiB per token: 0.75 GiB for one 32K-token request and 3.00 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can gpt-oss-20b run on a single H100?

Yes. As published in FP4 it fits on one H100 80 GB with 32K tokens of context.

Can I run gpt-oss-20b on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

On one RTX 4090 (24 GB) it fits as published with 32K of context. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it as published, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can gpt-oss-20b handle on 1 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 128K tokens (the model's limit is 128K). At 32K tokens per request, about 148 requests fit in memory at once.
