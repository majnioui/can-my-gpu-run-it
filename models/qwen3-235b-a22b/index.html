Can my GPU run Qwen3-235B-A22B?

Qwen3-235B-A22B has 235B parameters, about 22.2B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 438 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 8 × H100 80 GB, 4 × H200 141 GB, 2 × B300 288 GB and 16 × L40S 48 GB. Quantized to INT4, it fits on a single H200 141 GB, B200 180 GB or B300 288 GB.

Questions

How much VRAM does Qwen3-235B-A22B need?

The weights take 438 GiB as published (BF16), 438 GiB in BF16, 224 GiB in FP8 and 118 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 188.0 KiB per token: 5.88 GiB for one 32K-token request and 23.5 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can Qwen3-235B-A22B run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (118 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 8 H100s.

Can I run Qwen3-235B-A22B on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it in FP8, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can Qwen3-235B-A22B handle on 4 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 256K tokens (the model's limit is 256K). At 32K tokens per request, about 10 requests fit in memory at once.
