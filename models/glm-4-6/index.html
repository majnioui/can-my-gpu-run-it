Can my GPU run GLM-4.6?

GLM-4.6 has 357B parameters, about 37.6B active per token. As published in BF16 the weights take 657 GiB. With 32K tokens of context it needs 16 × H100 80 GB, 8 × H200 141 GB, 4 × B300 288 GB and more than 16 × L40S 48 GB. Quantized to INT4, it fits on a single B300 288 GB, MI355X 288 GB or Mac M3 Ultra 512 GB.

Questions

How much VRAM does GLM-4.6 need?

The weights take 657 GiB as published (BF16), 665 GiB in BF16, 340 GiB in FP8 and 178 GiB in INT4. On top of that, the KV cache adds about 368.0 KiB per token: 11.5 GiB for one 32K-token request and 46.0 GiB at 128K. Serving engines also need a few GiB per GPU for activations.

Can GLM-4.6 run on a single H100?

No. Even in INT4 the weights (178 GiB) don't fit in the ~70 GiB an H100 can use. As published it needs 16 H100s.

Can I run GLM-4.6 on an RTX 4090 or a Mac?

It doesn't fit on one RTX 4090 (24 GB), even in INT4. A Mac Studio with M3 Ultra and 512 GB runs it in INT4, for example a Q4 GGUF with llama.cpp or MLX.

How much context can GLM-4.6 handle on 8 × H200 141 GB?

One request can use up to 198K tokens (the model's limit is 198K). At 32K tokens per request, about 29 requests fit in memory at once.
