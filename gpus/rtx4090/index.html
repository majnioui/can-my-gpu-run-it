What can an RTX 4090 24 GB run?

The RTX 4090 has 24 GB of memory and 1.01 TB/s of bandwidth. It runs FP8 natively. On its own it runs Qwen3.5-9B, gpt-oss-20b and Llama-3.1-8B as published. Quantized to INT4, one also runs Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B, Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-8B.
